What counts as a good password here
  • At least 15 characters, at most 128.
  • Anything you can type: spaces, punctuation, accents, other alphabets, emoji. There are no rules about capitals, digits or symbols.
  • Nothing is truncated, and this password never expires.
  • Passwords found in public breach corpora are refused, and so is one that contains your username.
  • Four or five ordinary words is the easy way to pass — and it beats a short complicated one.

15 characters or more. A passphrase is fine.
